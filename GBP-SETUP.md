# GBL HVAC LTD: Google Business Profile Setup

Everything needed to create the Google Business Profile (GBP). Fields are in the order Google asks for them.
Services and descriptions match the website. If something changes on the site, change it here and on the profile too.

> **Golden rule:** the name, phone and service area must match the website **character for character**.
> A mismatch is the most common reason a profile gets suspended or an edit gets rejected.

---

## 2. Categories

Pick **one primary** and **up to 9 secondary** categories. Type each one into the category box and choose it **only if it appears in Google's dropdown with the same wording**. Google's list changes, and some names below may be worded slightly differently.

**Primary (most important ranking signal):**
- `HVAC contractor`

**Secondary, in priority order (all match services on the website):**

| Category | Why | Website page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Heating contractor` | Furnaces, boilers | /heating |
| `Furnace repair service` | Core winter search, 24/7 | /heating, /heating/24-hour-furnace-repair |
| `Air conditioning contractor` | AC installation | /air-conditioning |
| `Air conditioning repair service` | AC repair | /air-conditioning |
| `Commercial refrigeration` | Walk-ins, ice machines, coolers | /refrigeration |
| `Refrigeration repair service` | If it appears | /refrigeration |
| `Water heater installation service` | If it appears | /water-heaters |
| `Ventilating equipment` / `Air duct` options | **Only** a repair or install type, never "cleaning" or "manufacturer" | /ventilation |

**Avoid these categories:**
- Anything ending in **store, supplier, wholesaler or manufacturer**. Those mean selling products.
- `Air duct cleaning service`. He doesn't advertise duct cleaning.
- `Appliance repair service` or `Refrigerator repair service`. Those are for home fridges and washers.
- `Plumber` or `Gas company`. Don't add these unless Quinn confirms he holds the licence and wants to be found for it.

Fewer accurate categories beat many loose ones.

2. **Add custom services** for everything below. Put each under the category shown, then paste the description.
3. **Leave the price blank.** No pricing has been confirmed.

## 8. Verification

Google decides the method. For trades it is usually a **video recording** made on a phone in one continuous take:
1. Start outside: show the street and the **branded van** (name and phone number clearly visible).
2. Show **tools and equipment** (gauges, vacuum pump, ladder, parts).
3. Show **proof of management**: unlock the van, or show a business document or the business licence.

Keep it under about 2 minutes. Don't edit or cut the video. Approval can take a few days. **Don't change the name, address or categories while verification is pending.**
